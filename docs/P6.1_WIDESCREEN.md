# P6.1 Widescreen (field and battle)

Status: **second pass (preview.22 OLED feedback applied).** Built early
because the owner asked; it does not start Phase 6. **On by default**
(`DW3_WIDESCREEN=0` boots in 4:3). Validated on PC and Ryubing with the US
and EU discs; see **OLED feedback** for what changed.

## What it does

With the Port options **Widescreen** toggle on (the default), the game
draws past the left and right edges of its 320×240 picture:

| Mode | Gutter per side | Picture | How the gutters are filled |
|---|---|---|---|
| Battle (`FIGHTSTG`) | 53 px | 426×240, about 16:9 | 3D; the mesh culling already keeps a 64 px margin |
| Field (`FIELDSTG`) | 53 px | 426×240, about 16:9 | a fifth tile column in 3 extra tile slots (patch 0052) |
| Field menu, item shop, memory card, partner name | 53 px | 426×240 | their 96-px scrolling pattern drawn twice more (patch 0053) |
| Title screen (`STDWTITL`, while its backdrop is drawn) | 53 px | 426×240 | the backdrop mirrored about the 4:3 edges (patch 0085) |
| Player name (`STPLNMET`, the opening's Digimon Online registration) | 53 px | 426×240 | the hexagon pattern continued by whole periods, side lines carried out (patch 0087) |
| Anything else | 0 | 320×240, 4:3 | lab, gym, other menus, movies, the still screen, card game |

The HUD and menus stay where the game puts them, in the middle 320 columns.

## How

The PS1 framebuffers sit at VRAM (0,0) and (0,256), and the game keeps UI
textures at x=320, so the picture cannot grow inside VRAM. The extra columns
live outside it, as **side gutters**:

- `Psyz_VideoSetGutter(px)` (PSY-Z, `include/psyz/video.h`). A drawing area
  exactly as wide as the display, the full height or any band of its rows
  (PSY-Z `dw3-gutter-width`, 2026-10-23; it used to need the full height
  too), draws `px` more columns on each side of those rows. An untextured fill of the whole area (the DRAWENV background clear)
  covers the gutters, a display-wide `ClearImage` clears them, and a buffer
  flip blackens the gutters of the buffer drawn next, so layers that never
  reach the gutters (transitions with a smaller clip) leave them black
  rather than stale. Present shows gutter + display + gutter with the aspect
  widened by `(320 + 2·px) / 320`.
- PC `sdl3_gpu`: each batch that draws to the display is drawn a second time
  into a VRAM-sized gutter texture, shifted by `px`. Present copies the
  display into the middle of that texture and shows all of it. Works with
  `DW3_PC_INTERNAL_RES`.
- Switch: PSY-Z's CPU rasterizer writes gutter pixels into two planes
  (`SoftwareRasterizer_*Gutter*`); `switch_gpu.c` owns the setting. The battle
  mesh FBO (`nx_mesh.c`) gets a second gutter FBO the same way the PC does.
  `nx_gpu.c` packs gutter + VRAM + gutter (`dw3_nx_pack_rgb555_wide`) into
  its own texture and draws it, mesh first as before.
- Policy (`port/options/widescreen.c`): once per frame, before `drawFrame`
  (patch 0047), pick the gutter for the current mode overlay.
- Game patches, active only while the gutter is non-zero:
  0048 keeps the field camera far enough from the map edge that the gutters
  show map, not black, and draws map objects inside the gutters; 0049
  widens battle mesh culling by the gutter; 0051 makes the full-screen
  fades, darkness, white flash and field cover span the gutters; 0052 is
  the 16:9 field (below) and centers maps too narrow for both gutters;
  0053 widens the menu backgrounds; 0074 does the same for the battle report; 0075
  carries the field menu's block dissolve (into and out of the Items menu and
  the other field menu screens) across the gutters; 0076 moves the field
  menu's and its screens' edge panels out to the 16:9 edges (below, **Items
  menu**); 0085 fills the title screen's gutters, 0086 widens the field
  message box (the opening's news captions) and 0087 the player name screen
  (below, **Title, news caption and registration screen**); 0071 widens the area name banner's
  bars and sweep lines (its background and its top/bottom close reach the
  gutters through the band rule above); 0072 stretches a battle 2D effect
  frame that spans the whole 320-wide screen (the blue band and
  magic-circle backdrop at the start of a technique) across the gutters.
- 16:9 field (0052). A 53-px gutter needs 5×3 tiles, 15 images, but the
  game has 12 tile slots (x 640–1023). The host adds 3 in VRAM that field
  mode leaves unused (checked on the US and EU discs: no loads, no tpage or
  CLUT reference): (448,0), (448,128) and (576,0), with CLUTs on rows
  0xFC–0xFE. Only a 64-unit strip is left at 576, and tile images can be
  128 units wide, so that last slot holds the image's left half at (576,0)
  and its right half 128 rows under it; its sprites are remapped (and
  split when they cross the halves) when drawn. With a gutter of 32 or
  less only the 12 stock slots are used.

See [P6.1_WIDESCREEN_MENU_AUDIT.md](P6.1_WIDESCREEN_MENU_AUDIT.md) for a
screen-by-screen list of which menus and transitions are adapted and what
the rest need.

## Known limits

- Panels the menus park just past the old 4:3 edge show their cut ends
  in the gutters (shop and partner name side panels). The field menu and
  its screens end at the 16:9 edges since patch 0076 (see **Items menu**
  below); the Map screen, the battle results' header and the memory card
  since patches 0080-0082 (see **Map, results and save screens**). A field UI panel parked
  past the left edge
  shows as a 1-px line (slides into the gutter near save points).
- Lab, gym and the card game stay 4:3. (The battle report is 16:9 since
  patch 0074, the title screen and the player name screen since 0085 and
  0087; see below.)
- Area-change transitions: the iris (`FIELDSTG_closeField`) and its blue
  grid cover, and the encounter spiral (`FIELDSTG_playBattleTransition`),
  are still 320 wide (details in the menu audit). On PC `sdl3_gpu` only, the
  map shows again after the iris closes: an empty drawing area (a 0x0
  layer clip) keeps the previous scissor instead of drawing nothing. (Since
  PSY-Z `dw3-gutter-flush` the primitives queued before such an area are at
  least drawn with their own area, so they reach the gutters; see patch
  0086 below.)
- Anything the game culls by its own screen test, other than the two
  patched above, can pop in at the gutter edge. Field actors and battle
  sprites have no such test in the decomp; this has not been checked in game.
- 2D backgrounds the game draws only 320 wide stay 320 wide; the gutters
  beside them are black. That is why only the menus above take gutters.
- The PC window keeps its default 4:3 size; widescreen shows letterboxed in
  it until the window is resized or fullscreen.

### Items menu (field menu) (2026-10-24)

The rc8 OLED report (owner, US, 16:9): opening the Items menu, the opening
transition stays 4:3; once inside, the picture is 16:9 but the menu is still
a 4:3 layout.

How it is drawn (PC US and EU, `items-menu` replay, dense captures):

- Start in a field opens the field menu panels (`engine/menu/field_menu.c`,
  `updateFieldMenu`) over the field, in `FIELDSTG` (gutter 53). Picking Items
  (or Sort, Map, ...) runs `stepFieldMenuFade` / `drawFieldMenuFade`: six
  screen-size sprites of `FILE_MENU_SPRITES` (`FIELD_MENU_SPRITES` 29..34)
  at (0,0), each a set of 64x64 blocks (five per 320-pixel row) stepped
  through darker CLUT rows two frames at a time, then `requestMode(MODE_STATUS)`.
  The menu mode is the overlay `STSTATUS` (US `0x1F9`, EU `0x207`), already in
  the gutter policy (53 px) with its 96-pixel background drawn three times
  (0053). Triangle back to the field runs the same dissolve inside `STSTATUS`.
- The dissolve's sprites covered only the 320 middle columns, so the field
  (into the menu) or the menu background (out of it) stayed lit in both
  gutters while the middle went black: a 4:3 frame around the transition.
  That is the "opening transition stays 4:3". The background inside was
  already 16:9.
- Inside, every panel kept its PS1 position (until patch 0076, below): the party pages (`0x15`/`0x17`
  at x 0), money (`0x1A` at 0), help bar (`0x20` at 0, full width) and the
  Items list frame (`0x2E` at 0) are drawn from the left 4:3 edge, the option
  and list panels (`0x1C` at 0xA8, `0x26` at 0xA0, `0x3A` at 0xA8) and the
  title bar (`0x18`) run to the right 4:3 edge, and the panels open with a
  horizontal scale about pivots x 0 and 0x140, the old edges. In 16:9 their
  cut ends sit 53 px inside the picture and they grow out of those lines.
  That is "the menu remains a 4:3 layout".

Fix (patch `0075`): with the gutter non-zero, each dissolve sprite is drawn
twice more, at x -320 and +320; the blocks tile the 320 columns exactly, so
the gutters get the outer block columns of the copies and the dissolve runs
over the whole 16:9 picture, into and out of the menu. Panels unchanged (0076 moves them).

  and clamps it to 320; the blue grid is 320 wide (0051 widened the fade, not
  the grid). Fix: start the clip at 320+2*gutter centred on the player and
  widen the grid. Small patch, next free number.
- **Spiral.** `MoveImage` copies only the 320x240 display to VRAM x=0x280; the
  gutters are not in VRAM, so they go black at once. Either accept a black
  16:9 with a 4:3 swirl, or copy the gutter planes into the transition image
  in PSY-Z and extend the tiles and the darkening `POLY_F4` to 426 px.
- **PC `sdl3_gpu` bug (also in 4:3).** After the iris closes the map shows
  again, shifted: `closeField` leaves the map layer with a 0x0 clip (end <
  start), which draws nothing on the PS1 and on the Switch raster, but
  `UpdateScissor` in `sdl3_gpu.c` ignores an empty area and keeps the previous
  scissor.
- **Speed.** The PC runs the game's own frame counts: the banner is about 126
  frames (boxes stretch, names typed at delay 5, the wait for the map streamer,
  a 60-frame hold, a 15-frame close); the spiral about 98 frames, then 30
  black, then a 55-frame fade-in. On the Switch wall time can be longer for
  two reasons that are not told apart yet: the port is frame-locked
  (`frameTime` is always 1, so a missed vblank slows everything) and the
  60-frame hold starts only after the new map's tiles are read from the SD.
  An OLED nxlink log (`frame dt us=`, field tile reads) across a door and into
  a battle decides it. A shorter banner hold needs its own option (P6.18,
  owner's call).
- **Technique backdrop polish.** The stretch applies once the frame spans the
  screen, so a band that grows from the middle widens by the gutter factor in
  one frame at that point. Not seen as a problem in the captures (the band
  grows in a few frames); smooth it only if the OLED shows it.
- **Magic circle.** In Ryubing the rotated magic circle has a black hole in
  its centre. It is identical on main, so it does not come from `0072`; PC
  `sdl3_gpu` does not show it.
- **OLED.** Banner and technique backdrop on US and EU, and a look for other
  display-wide band layers that now draw into the gutters (the new PSY-Z rule
  applies to any drawing area as wide as the display, whatever its height).

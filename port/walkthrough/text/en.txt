wt.area_west.help = Byakko City and the West Sector come in this order: the Underground Cave, the Mirage Room (Byakko Leader), the Operation Room (HiAndromon), then the Sewers and the Secret Room, the rebels' hideout off the Sewers. The Underground Cave is visited again later.
